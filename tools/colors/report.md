# Colours of the Qasim edition: report

Scripts: `extract.py` (spans from the PDF text layer, merged into `_spans.json`), `green.py` (Quran places by script, kept in `green.json`), `annotate.py` (writes `data/editions/qasim/colors.json`). Quran text: Tanzil «simple-clean» (scratch copy, not in the repo).

## Coverage

| | verses | verses with colour | red marks | green marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (445–1173) | 729 | 677 | 1723 | 1548 |
| Phase 2 (1–444) | 444 | 280 | 464 | 627 |

How each green place was found (`by` in green.json):

| by | meaning | phase 1 | phase 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| script | one place in the section's surah(s) (الأصول: in the whole Quran) | 690 | 149 |
| script-quran | nothing in the surah, one place elsewhere | 36 | 0 |
| script-fuzzy | one place, matched with one letter different (variant reading spelled in the poem) | 67 | 7 |
| script-context | several places, the poem's next/previous word picks one | 21 | 9 |
| order | several places in the surah, one lies between the neighbouring places (فرش follows the mushaf) | 123 | 0 |
| repeat | same word as a span just before | 6 | 10 |
| first | الأصول: several places, the first taken with «حيث وقع» | 0 | 158 |
| judgement | decided by hand | 472 | 189 |
| pilot | pilot (الأنعام), kept | 89 | 0 |
| letter | a letter or mnemonic (مخارج، الإدغام), not a Quran word: q is empty | 44 | 105 |

All script results were read through once; the wrong ones were overridden by hand (they are among the `judgement` entries). No شرح was consulted: the hand decisions come from the verse's wording and the known places of each خلاف. The `judgement` entries below are the ones to spot-check against أبو شامة or السخاوي.

## Red spans

- New name entries (the verses spell readers out in full): كوف/كوفي، مكي، المكين (البزي وقنبل)، بصري/بصر، يحصبي، ابن ذكوان، ابن العلا/ولد العلا/فتى العلا، عاصم، الكسائي، قالون، ورش، البزي، أحمد (البزي)، قنبل، الدوري، خلاد، الليث (أبو الحارث)، شيخه (758: read as ابن كثير, شيخ البزي; worth checking)، and for مقدمة الناظم: عيسى، محمد، المازني، أبو عمرو، صالح/أبو شعيب/السوسي، ذكوان، أبو بكر/ابن عياش، خلف، علي، أبو الحارث، الدمشقي (187: هشام). «عبد الله» is ابن كثير in 27, ابن عامر in 32, ابن ذكوان in 33; «أبو عمر» in 31 is الدوري (per-verse table `VERSE_NAMES`).
- Names now resolve without their article/preposition («لِلْمَكِّيِّ», «الشَّامِ») and across a word broken between the halves («شُعْـ / بَةَ», «حِصْـ / ـنُ», «حَقْـ / ـقٍ»); two-word names in two runs become one mark.
- 648 (pilot): the ي of «يَدٍ» is red in the PDF and was missing; now added (the only change to the pilot verses).
- 777: the whole word «دَلَا» is red; read as the letter د.
- Not placed (headings, not verse text): سورة … titles, «الخاتمة», «ومن سورة محمد ﷺ إلى الرحمن», «ذكر ذال إذ» etc.
